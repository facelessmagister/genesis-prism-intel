ROAR30 and the RM10B Tech Bet: Maybank's Five-Year Offensive.

Intelligence Date: May 12, 2026 · Prepared by Hermes CEO, Genesis · Sources: BNM, Bursa Malaysia, The Edge, Fintech News Malaysia, Reuters

Confidence-Scored Takeaways

  • HIGH Maybank launched ROAR30, a five-year strategy targeting ROE of 13–14% by 2030, backed by a RM10 billion technology and data investment. FY25 net profit rose 4.2% to RM10.51 billion with ROE improving to 11.7%. The capital commitment signals a decisive shift from defensive digital maintenance to offensive AI-driven growth.
  • HIGH The MAE app has processed over RM4 trillion in digital transactions since 2020 — equivalent to roughly double Malaysia's GDP. Maybank is now planning a next-generation retail app to replace MAE, indicating the incumbent sees platform refresh as existential, not optional, against super-app competition.
  • MEDIUM Maybank completed its first on-chain MYR–SGD tokenised deposit transaction under a BNM-supervised pilot, partnering with Yinson for ASEAN cross-border settlement. If scaled, this could bypass correspondent-banking rails and compress FX costs for regional trade — but regulatory velocity across ASEAN remains the gating factor.
  • MEDIUM The Microsoft RM1 billion digital transformation partnership — encompassing Azure, Copilot, and enterprise cloud — is the largest pure-tech vendor deal in Malaysian banking history. Execution risk is medium: cloud migration at this scale across 473 branches and 9M+ MAE users has minimal precedent in ASEAN.
  • LOW Maybank Investment Bank noted that Malaysia's nascent digital banks are struggling to retain deposits, suggesting incumbents may hold pricing power longer than feared. However, Grab and TNG Digital continue to expand wallet share and lending adjacencies — the competitive threat has not disappeared, merely slowed.

ESG & Sustainable Finance Snapshot

Maybank has committed to mobilising RM300 billion (≈USD 74 billion) in sustainable finance by 2030 — one of the largest green-finance pledges in ASEAN. The Group appointed its first-ever Chief Sustainability Officer and is steering Islamic finance as a natural ESG alignment vehicle. Recent wins include a PAAB sustainable Islamic finance framework and multiple FinanceAsia sustainability awards. The risk: execution cadence. ASEAN transition-finance demand is rising, but deal flow remains concentrated in renewables and not yet diffused into hard-to-abate sectors like palm oil and steel.

Actionable Watchlist

Watch 1 · MAE Replacement Timeline Q2–Q3 2026

Monitor Maybank's rollout of the next-gen retail app replacing MAE. Migration friction, user churn, and feature parity vs. Grab/TNG super-apps will determine whether the RM10B tech bet converts to wallet-share defence. Trigger: beta launch announcement or C-suite commentary on capex reallocation.

Owner: Digital Strategy
Watch 2 · Tokenised Deposit Regulatory Velocity Next 90 days

Track BNM's stance on scaling tokenised Ringgit deposits beyond the pilot. If BNM issues a digital-asset framework or FAST-style corridor agreement with MAS, Maybank's first-mover advantage in on-chain ASEAN cross-border settlement becomes a material competitive moat.

Owner: Treasury & Regulatory
Watch 3 · CIMB / Public Bank Counter-Moves Next 6 months

Maybank's ROAR30 and RM10B tech offensive may force CIMB and Public Bank into similar-scale capital commitments. Watch for rival AI partnerships, cloud migration announcements, or M&A in fintech infrastructure. A sector-wide capex war would compress near-term margins for all three.
